🎯 How to Use This Hub

New to Sitar? Start with "15 Learning Challenges" to get the big picture, then dive into "Bilawal Alankars Plan" for your first month of structured practice.

Want to understand theory? Begin with "Composition Sections Guide" and "Thaat System Overview," then progress to the complete 12-Month Theory Course.

Ready to play music? Learn "Prabhat Raga" as your first piece, then work toward "Shaam-e-Safar" for performance.

Pro Tip: Practice and theory should go hand-in-hand. Spend 70% time on technique/pieces, 30% on theory study.

Why These Resources Work For You

Every resource here acknowledges your tabla expertise. You're not a "beginner musician" - you're an advanced rhythmic musician learning melodic expression. These materials leverage what you already know:

  • Your understanding of taal, sam, and rhythmic cycles
  • Your knowledge of composition structure (theme, variation, development)
  • Your performance experience and stage presence
  • Your ear training from accompanying raags for years
  • Your discipline and practice methodology from tabla training

This isn't learning from scratch - it's translation from rhythm to melody. You'll progress faster than typical beginners because you're building on solid musical foundation!
